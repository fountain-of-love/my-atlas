# Dances

This document is a shared placeholder for movements that begin to coexist as the Atlas passes through its tensions. These are observations, not established dances or a finished architecture.

## Process

The current process is:

`Intent → Field → Direction → Tension → Dance → …`

The process itself is part of the model. We are applying this evolution while observing and documenting it.

## Movements currently observed

### Creation

Intuition → prototyping or creating → outcome → feedback → movement.

Creation moves outward and gives the field something concrete to respond to.

### Containment

Guiding, correcting, setting boundaries, and introducing structure. Governance is already beginning to appear here.

Containment currently appears less like a separate dance and more like the boundary or proto-governance within which other movements can remain coherent.

### Reflection

Observing the learning curve, capturing lessons, noticing recurring behaviour, and documenting emerging patterns.

Reflection turns activity into knowledge without assuming that the knowledge is final.

### Facilitation

Keeping the process moving without rushing toward crystallisation. In particular, resisting the tendency—especially with AI—to jump directly to specifications and an apparently finished system.

Facilitation holds the field open so that recurring form can emerge through movement rather than be imposed in advance.

## Emerging mediators

Creation, Facilitation, and Reflection appear to be emerging as possible mediators between tensions and form. Containment appears to mediate at another level: as the boundary or proto-governance that allows the other movements to remain coherent.

These are provisional observations. They should be tested against future work rather than promoted into a fixed structure now.

## Interactions to observe

- Creation produces outcomes that Reflection can interpret and Facilitation can keep in motion.
- Reflection can inform Containment by revealing where boundaries, corrections, or structure are useful.
- Containment can protect Creation and Facilitation from both premature crystallisation and uncontrolled drift.
- Facilitation can make room for Creation and Reflection to alternate, overlap, or form new threads.
- The relationship between outward Expression and inward Reflection may become clearer through repeated cycles of making and learning.

## Evidence from repository-building

The repository is already showing the process it describes. Concepts first appeared in the README, then became named placeholders as their distinctness became meaningful. `Approach` was renamed to `Direction`, while the former `Direction` became `Tensions`, preserving the learning rather than forcing the earlier framing to remain stable.

This renaming is evidence of the Atlas observing and correcting its own emerging structure.

## Unresolved questions

- Do Creation, Facilitation, and Reflection become recurring dances, or are they temporary descriptions of this phase?
- Is Containment best understood as a mediator, a boundary condition, a governance layer, or something else?
- Do the threads Emergence ↔ Containment and Expression ↔ Reflection remain useful when more repository evidence accumulates?
- What other movements appear when the Atlas is used by someone beyond its creator?
- When does a recurring pattern deserve its own artifact, and when should it remain within this shared field?
