# Source

This is the canonical source workspace for the self-model.

The source is organized from a person perspective rather than from the structure of a CV. It begins with who Yves is and how he tends to think, create, decide, learn, and relate. Experiences, projects, education, and other contexts provide evidence and texture around that centre.
