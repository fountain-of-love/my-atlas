# Contexts

Contexts are the situations through which the person-first source becomes observable and testable.

They include experiences, projects, formal education, learning, people, and organizations. They are supporting branches, not the organizing definition of the person.

Formal education records the programmes and qualifications. Learning is
organised by the knowledge areas Yves has acquired. Its category pages
interpret patterns across education and course evidence; individual sources
belong in the evidence branch and are linked back from the relevant context.
