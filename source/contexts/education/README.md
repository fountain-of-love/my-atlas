# Formal education

This branch records the formal programmes Yves followed or is reported to
have followed. It answers what the programme was and where it belongs in his
educational trajectory.

It is deliberately separate from [learning](../learning/README.md), which
interprets what knowledge areas and ways of thinking may have been acquired.
It is also separate from [education evidence](../../evidence/education/README.md),
which preserves source material and current programme descriptions.

## Education contexts

- [Commercial Engineer — KU Leuven](commercial-engineer-kuleuven.md) — bachelor and master trajectory, with the reported biotechnology and human-machine communication minors.
- [Graduate Programming — UCLL](graduate-programming-ucll.md) — reported programming qualification associated with an evening trajectory.
- [Master of Artificial Intelligence — KU Leuven](master-artificial-intelligence-kuleuven.md) — reported advanced academic AI programme.

## Evidence state

These records currently combine a subject/profile claim with a current
institutional programme description. The current programme pages provide
context, not a perfect reconstruction of the historical curriculum. Completion
dates, exact historical titles, specialisations, and completion status remain
open unless directly evidenced.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `claimed` | Yves or a profile communicates that he followed or obtained it |
| `programme-context` | The institution describes the current programme |
| `verified` | A diploma, transcript, alumni record, or direct confirmation supports the historical claim |
