# Process mining and operational insight

This learning context gathers Yves's acquisition of methods for understanding
how work actually flows through systems and for comparing that flow with an
intended process. It sits between data analysis, process improvement, and
operational decision support.

The category is intentionally narrow: it is backed by a specific course and
should not be expanded into a broader process-improvement capability without
additional evidence.

## What was learned or acquired

- Process-mining concepts and event-log-based analysis.
- A foundation for discovering, checking, and improving operational processes
  through data.
- The relationship between process evidence and data-science action.

## Course evidence

The [LinkedIn education and course record](../../evidence/education/linkedin-education.md)
lists Process Mining: Data science in Action from Coursera, issued January
2015.

## What would strengthen this context

Connect the learning to process models, bottleneck analysis, operational
improvements, or decisions made from observed process data.
