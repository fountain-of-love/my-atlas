# Experience specification

An experience entry should answer enough of these questions to make the experience meaningful and evidence-bearing.

## Optional metadata shape

```yaml
type: experience
organization: "[[Organization]]"
role: ""
start: "YYYY-MM"
end: "YYYY-MM or present"
domains: []
projects: []
skills: []
evidence: []
```

The fields are a starting point, not a required universal schema.

## Identity

- What organization, team, role, and location were involved?
- When did it begin and end?

## Context

- What was the situation or problem?
- What constraints, people, or systems mattered?

## Contribution

- What did Yves personally do, decide, guide, or own?

## Outcomes

- What changed?
- What was delivered or learned?

## Evidence

- Which projects, artefacts, metrics, references, or public traces support the account?

## Reflection

- What capability did this demonstrate?
- What was learned?
- What would be done differently?
