# Process

This is the practical process for developing the Atlas while keeping the field open.

## Current sequence

`Intent → Field → Direction → Tension → Dance → …`

The sequence is a working grammar rather than a fixed pipeline. The process itself is part of the model: we are applying this evolution while observing and documenting it.

When existing material is being brought into the Atlas, use the more specific [ingestion loop](INGESTION.md) rather than copying source documents directly into the self-model.

## The fishbone

The repository structure acts as a fishbone: a concrete surface against which ideas, facts, experiments, and questions can be placed. The person-first source is the central perspective; contexts and evidence are branches that help us observe and test what is true. It is useful because it gives direction without claiming that the final system is already known.

We can use it to:

- place new material in a provisional person or context domain;
- test whether a domain needs its own concepts or relationships;
- try different entry shapes;
- compare domain-specific specifications;
- discover connections across domains;
- generate outputs from increasingly useful signal.

## Working loop

1. **Gather** lived experience, intuition, artefacts, decisions, and questions.
2. **Place** the material against the fishbone, using the nearest provisional person or context domain.
3. **Shape** it into a human-readable entry with explicit metadata where useful.
4. **Relate** it to concepts, domains, projects, capabilities, outcomes, and evidence.
5. **Reflect** on what the material reveals, including uncertainty and contradiction.
6. **Contain** by correcting, selecting, naming, and integrating without closing too early.
7. **Express** a view for a concrete audience or purpose.
8. **Feed back** the result and its shortcomings into the next pass.

The loop is intentionally lightweight. It should increase signal before it increases formalism.

## Progressive formalization

An entry may begin as a note, become a structured placeholder, and later earn a domain-specific specification. The required structure should grow in response to actual use rather than being imposed universally at the beginning.

## Human and machine readability

Keep prose as the primary surface for human understanding. Add lightweight front matter when explicit fields help tools discover, compare, filter, or relate entries. The metadata should point into the shared dictionary rather than inventing an isolated vocabulary.

## AI-paradigm demonstration

The Atlas is also a demonstration of a way of working with AI: keep human intent, lived knowledge, judgment, and boundary decisions in the loop while using Enigma for synthesis, structure, challenge, retrieval, transformation, and useful disturbance.

The process should make this collaboration inspectable rather than hiding it behind polished outputs.
