# Atlas System

This document collects observations that may become part of the reusable system emerging from My Atlas.

The system is explicitly provisional. It should not be crystallised yet. We first need to spend enough time moving through the tensions and observing whether recurring dances, mediators, boundaries, and practices become stable enough to name.

## Consistency principle

Meaningful elements of the atlas system should be represented consistently. If an element becomes a distinct concept, layer, or practice, it should receive an explicit, named artifact of its own—rather than being described only as embedded text in another element.

The README should provide orientation and show the relationships between elements. The element’s own document should hold its description, current understanding, and future development. When an element is still emerging, create a clearly named placeholder rather than losing the insight or prematurely pretending it is complete.

This keeps the emerging system inspectable, extensible, and reusable by others without pretending that its architecture is already known.

## Current stance

The atlas is applying its own evolutionary process while documenting it. System-relevant observations may be collected here, but they remain hypotheses until repeated movement provides stronger evidence.

The inherited field is kept together under [foundations](foundations/README.md). Those documents provide the vocabulary, lenses, tensions, and working grammar from which the current system is emerging; this document records what is beginning to exceed that bootstrap layer.

## Harvested observations

### The system is self-describing

The Atlas is not only describing Yves; it is also describing how the description is being made. Its process, choices, corrections, and history are themselves part of the model.

This creates a recursive or fractal quality: the same principles can appear in the subject, the content, the document structure, and the repository history.

### The repository is both artefact and evidence

The repository is not merely a container for the model. Its files, names, links, edits, renamings, and commits are evidence of the model’s evolution.

The system may therefore need to preserve two kinds of truth:

- what the Atlas currently says about Yves;
- what the Atlas’s own evolution reveals about how Yves thinks and works.

### The README is a map, not the whole territory

The README provides orientation, sequence, and relationships between emerging elements. Distinct elements receive their own documents when they become meaningful enough to name.

This creates a useful separation between navigation and depth without forcing every concept to be fully specified when it first appears.

### A placeholder is an epistemic state

A placeholder is not an unfinished failure or an empty file. It marks something that has been observed and named, while deliberately leaving its final form open.

This allows the Atlas to preserve valuable discoveries without confusing recognition with completion.

### Naming is an act of observation

Renaming `Approach` to `Direction` and the earlier `Direction` to `Tensions` showed that names are hypotheses about the shape of the field. When experience reveals a better distinction, the system should be able to revise its names without treating the earlier model as wasted work.

The history of such revisions is valuable evidence, not noise to hide.

### The system develops through tension

The Atlas should not resolve tensions by selecting one pole too early. Tensions create pressure, and that pressure may reveal new movements, distinctions, or forms.

This suggests a system behaviour of holding, revisiting, and learning from tensions before turning them into rules.

### Crystallisation should be earned

A recurring idea should not become a permanent structure merely because it can be named. Repetition, interaction, evidence, and usefulness should precede crystallisation.

The system therefore needs a distinction between:

- an observation;
- a hypothesis or emerging pattern;
- a recurring practice;
- a sufficiently tested structure.

The current Atlas is intentionally operating mostly in the first two states.

### The process has a temporal grammar

The sequence `Intent → Field → Direction → Tension → Dance → …` is not yet an architecture, but it is already a useful grammar for describing how the work unfolds.

It suggests that the system should preserve sequence and transition, not only a static collection of concepts. What happens next may be informed by what has emerged before, while remaining open to a different movement.

### Multiple audiences are part of the design

The Atlas has a personal purpose—presenting Yves through his work—and a reusable purpose—helping others build inspectable self-models.

These are not separate products yet. Their tension may be generative: personal specificity supplies truth and evidence, while reusability encourages clarity, explicitness, and transferable patterns.

### Quality is a movement, not only a threshold

High standards and forward movement are held together through iteration. Creation produces an outcome; feedback exposes its qualities and limits; reflection captures learning; containment introduces proportion and correction; the next movement begins.

Quality may therefore be better understood as a loop of refinement than as a final gate applied at the end.

### Containment is likely boundary work

Containment has appeared as guiding, correcting, setting boundaries, introducing structure, and protecting the field from premature crystallisation or uncontrolled drift.

This may become governance, but it should not yet be treated as a formal governance layer. For now, it is a boundary function to observe across the dances.

### Facilitation protects emergence

Facilitation is not passive waiting. It keeps movement possible while resisting the pressure to jump directly from an insight to a specification.

In an AI-assisted process, this may be especially important: the ability to generate a polished structure quickly can conceal whether the structure has actually been earned by observation.

### Compression should preserve generative detail

The Fibonacci- and nature-inspired blueprint seeks natural compression: expressing much through a small number of patterns. The system should compress repetition into useful form without erasing the tensions, exceptions, and evidence that make the model truthful.

### Public usefulness requires boundaries

The ambition to create a public and reusable self-model introduces an exposure ↔ privacy boundary. Reusability does not mean total disclosure. The system may eventually need ways to distinguish what is public, contextual, private, provisional, or evidence-sensitive.

This is an observation about a needed capability, not a proposed access-control architecture.

### One person-first source can support many expressions

The person-first self-model should be canonical while its outputs remain plural. A CV, website, profile, or LLM interaction may select and compress different aspects for different audiences without becoming a competing source of truth.

This makes the repository useful both as a living knowledge base and as a generator of practical representations.

### The Atlas needs two kinds of lineage

The education work exposed a distinction that should become a reusable system
principle. The first lineage is source-facing:

```text
source → extraction → evidence → context → claim
```

It preserves where a statement came from and what the source can support. The
second is person-facing:

```text
context → formative contribution → tension or reinforcement → capability → identity
```

It interprets how education, work, entrepreneurship, and learning may have
contributed to who Yves is now. The two lineages must be linked, but a source
claim must not be mistaken for a developmental explanation. LinkedIn can say
that a course was listed; it cannot by itself say how important that course was
to Yves's identity.

### Formative weight is asymmetric

The recent education model shows that items in a source register do not have
equal developmental weight. A full-time master’s education may provide a
slow-decaying conceptual foundation. A short tool course may have fast-decaying
tool knowledge but leave a durable habit or exposure. An entrepreneurial period
may shape judgement more deeply than either while being harder to represent as
a credential.

This suggests qualitative developmental metadata rather than a flat list:
formative role, formative weight, decay profile, transfer, and reinforcement.
These values belong to interpretation and remain provisional.

### Tensions are capability generators

The person-facing work surfaced theory ↔ practice, builder ↔ architect,
autonomy ↔ organisational responsibility, proximity ↔ scale, and breadth ↔
depth. The system should preserve these as fields of development rather than
flattening them into labels. A candidate capability becomes stronger when it is
repeatedly shaped by both poles and supported by outcomes.

### Dance is discovery; system is routing

The work reached these distinctions through a dance: resonance, contrast,
reframing, and recognition. The reusable system does not need to reproduce the
dance literally. It can use the resulting learnings as routing rules:

1. preserve the source observation;
2. route it to the correct context or evidence register;
3. identify its possible formative role and decay;
4. connect it to relevant tensions and reinforcing contexts;
5. deepen it only when it changes a capability or identity hypothesis;
6. compress it into an output while retaining a path back to evidence.

The dance remains a discovery method. The system becomes effective by making
the discovered distinctions explicit, reusable, and inspectable.

### Compression should be weighted, not uniform

Efficient synthesis does not mean shortening everything equally. It means
allocating attention according to lineage strength, formative weight,
reinforcement, uncertainty, and present relevance. Rich foundational contexts
and productive tensions deserve deeper treatment; low-weight or fast-decaying
items should remain concise but traceable.

### The person is the perspective, not another category

The initial scaffold was shaped too strongly by the composition of a CV. The emerging correction is to organize the source around the person: patterns, capabilities, identity, values, philosophy, ways of working, interests, and direction.

Experiences, projects, education, organizations, and relationships remain important, but as contexts in which the person becomes observable. They should help us discover and evidence who Yves is rather than define the full shape of who he can be.

### Domains may be heterogeneous

The fishbone can identify multiple domains, but the domains do not need to share one rigid schema. A pattern, capability, experience, project, relationship, or personal value may require different questions, relationships, evidence, and forms of interpretation.

The system may therefore need a family of local specifications connected by shared vocabulary rather than one universal record type.

### Vocabulary is infrastructure

A dictionary is not supplementary documentation. Shared definitions allow Yves, Enigma, readers, and future tools to mean similar things when using terms such as experience, capability, project, evidence, outcome, or learning.

Semantic alignment may be one of the system’s most important forms of interoperability.

### Structure can accelerate emergence

Structure is not necessarily containment in the restrictive sense. A fishbone, a domain map, and a lightweight spec can make it faster to place material, compare examples, notice gaps, and generate signal.

The test is whether structure increases movement and learning. If it mainly creates administrative overhead or false certainty, it has become too heavy.

### Specifications should be plural and earned

Different element types may need different specifications. Specifications should begin as useful questions, become lightweight contracts through repeated use, and gain formal detail only when real entries reveal a recurring need.

### The signal can precede perfection

The first useful threshold is not completeness. It is whether the model already communicates enough of who Yves is, what he can do, and what he can offer to support meaningful interaction and further learning.

This supports a progressive strategy: increase signal, preserve provenance, and improve precision over time.

### The repository demonstrates the method

The Atlas is itself evidence of how Yves approaches the AI paradigm shift: he supplies intent, lived knowledge, judgment, resonance, and boundaries; Enigma helps synthesize, structure, challenge, transform, and introduce disturbance.

The method is not merely described in the repository. It is made visible through the repository’s evolving structure and outputs.

## Possible system capabilities

The observations above suggest capabilities the system may eventually need. These are prompts for further exploration, not a component list:

- orient a reader across the emerging field;
- preserve the distinction between current belief, evidence, interpretation, and uncertainty;
- record the lineage of concepts as they are renamed, split, combined, or retired;
- connect claims about Yves to work, decisions, outcomes, and feedback;
- hold tensions without prematurely collapsing them into categories;
- allow multiple dances or movement threads to coexist;
- capture reflections and recurring patterns over time;
- make the boundary between public usefulness and personal privacy explicit;
- support natural compression while allowing a reader to follow the evidence back to richer detail;
- show both the self-model and the model-building process.
- preserve source lineage and developmental lineage as separate traversable paths;
- route evidence according to formative role, decay, reinforcement, and current relevance;
- identify productive tensions from which candidate capabilities may emerge;
- compress low-weight material without losing provenance or discoverability;
- distinguish a source-supported claim from a subject-supplied identity interpretation.

These capabilities should be validated by continued use. They should not yet be implemented as a prescribed architecture.

## Provisional operating principles

The following principles have been observed strongly enough to guide the next phase:

1. **Map before deepening.** Keep orientation visible while concepts develop in their own artifacts.
2. **Name without finalising.** A named placeholder can preserve an insight while keeping its form open.
3. **Let evidence change the model.** Repository activity and feedback are inputs to the ontology, not merely outputs of it.
4. **Hold tensions productively.** Do not resolve a tension until movement reveals what resolution, if any, is useful.
5. **Separate observation from crystallisation.** Record what is seen before deciding what it means structurally.
6. **Preserve lineage.** Renaming and revision are part of the model’s truth.
7. **Prefer earned abstraction.** Generalise only after recurring patterns have appeared in concrete work.
8. **Keep the process recursive.** Apply the same care to the way the Atlas evolves as to what it says about Yves.
