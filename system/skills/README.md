# Processing skills

These are reusable capabilities for moving material through the ingestion loop. They describe how the work is performed, not facts about Yves.

- [Source cartography](source-cartography.md) — understand what an input is and what perspective it carries.
- [Signal extraction](signal-extraction.md) — identify candidate person-level observations and claims.
- [Provenance weaving](provenance-weaving.md) — connect claims to sources, contexts, evidence, and uncertainty.
- [Developmental lineage](developmental-lineage.md) — interpret formative weight, decay, tension, reinforcement, and identity development without confusing interpretation with evidence.

They may later combine into a broader ingestion skill, but are kept separate while their boundaries are being tested.
