# Developmental lineage

## Purpose

Interpret how contexts may accumulate into capabilities and identity while
keeping that interpretation distinct from source evidence.

## Inputs

- validated or clearly labelled contexts;
- education, work, entrepreneurship, learning, and experience records;
- subject-supplied reflections about influence, sequence, contrast, or change;
- supporting evidence and unresolved questions.

## Produces

- formative roles and qualitative weights;
- decay and transfer hypotheses;
- reinforcement and transformation links between contexts;
- tension fields and candidate capabilities emerging from them;
- identity hypotheses with confidence and validation prompts.

## Working model

```text
context
  → formative contribution
    → tension or reinforcement
      → capability hypothesis
        → identity hypothesis
```

## Guardrails

- Do not infer formative weight from prestige alone.
- Do not treat a degree, course, role, or experience as equally formative by
  default.
- Separate tool-specific knowledge from durable habits, concepts, and identity
  contributions.
- Treat decay as transformation or reduced direct relevance, not automatic
  disappearance.
- Keep causal language qualified unless Yves or strong evidence supports it.
- Do not turn a tension into a capability until repeated contexts or outcomes
  provide support.
- Keep subject-supplied meaning distinct from public-source observation.

## Efficient routing

Use deeper analysis when an item is foundational, repeatedly reinforced,
strongly connected to a tension, or relevant to a current identity or
capability question. Keep low-weight, fast-decaying, or weakly connected items
in concise evidence registers with provenance intact.
