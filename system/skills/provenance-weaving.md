# Provenance weaving

## Purpose

Connect what the Atlas says to where it came from, what context shaped it, what evidence supports it, and how confident the interpretation should be.

## Produces

- source links and input IDs;
- claim–context–evidence–learning connections;
- cross-source comparisons;
- continuity, development, omission, and contradiction notes;
- uncertainty and validation prompts.

## Guardrails

- Do not merge conflicting sources silently.
- Do not confuse repetition with truth without considering source inheritance or copying.
- Preserve private or sensitive boundaries.
- Keep Yves’s validation distinct from Enigma’s interpretation.
