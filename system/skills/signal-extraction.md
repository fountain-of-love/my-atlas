# Signal extraction

## Purpose

Extract person-level signal from source material without converting the source directly into a CV or biography.

## Looks for

- recurring patterns of thought, action, creation, leadership, or learning;
- capabilities demonstrated in context;
- ways of working and decision tendencies;
- values, beliefs, interests, and direction;
- contexts, outcomes, and artefacts that make claims testable.

## Produces

- source observations;
- candidate claims;
- candidate patterns, capabilities, or ways of working;
- links to passages, artefacts, or contexts;
- alternative interpretations where the signal is ambiguous.

## Guardrails

- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Do not infer a stable trait from one isolated statement.
- Keep contradictory or incomplete signals visible.
- Prefer signal with provenance over polished generalization.
