# Source cartography

## Purpose

Map an input before extracting from it: what it is, when and why it was created, who it was for, what it emphasizes, and what it cannot tell us.

## Inputs

- one source item or a coherent source set;
- provenance and access context;
- the current input inventory.

## Produces

- an input registration;
- a short source profile;
- likely signal areas;
- known limitations, audience effects, and privacy boundaries.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat a source’s structure as the structure of Yves.
- Do not rank newer as automatically truer or older as automatically deeper.
- Preserve the source’s situated perspective.
